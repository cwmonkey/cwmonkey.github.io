Usage and description

Install the Greasemonkey script below to add a way to skip specific amounts of time in a video OR change the playback speed, in conjunction with bookmarklets.

After installing the script, drag any of these bookmarklets to your bookmarks bar (or make your own):

  • 2x - 2x playback speed
  • 4x - 4x playback speed
  • 8x - 8x playback speed
  • 16x - 16x playback speed (max)
  • 1x - 1x playback speed
  • 1 - skip forward 1 second
  • 10 - skip forward 10 seconds
  • 60
  • 5m
  • .03 - skip forward 1 frame (@30fps)
  • .016 - skip forward 1 frame (@60fps)
  • .03 - skip forward 1 frame (@30fps)
  • 5% - skip forward 5% of the video's total duration
  • 10%
  • 24%
  • -1 - skip backward 1 second
  • -10 - skip backward 10 seconds
  • -60
  • -5m
  • -.03 - skip backward 1 frame (@30fps)
